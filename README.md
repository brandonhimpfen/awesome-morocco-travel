Morocco is a captivating North African destination blending historic imperial cities, Atlas mountain ranges, and Sahara desert landscapes with rich Moorish heritage and Atlantic coasts. This list is intended for independent travelers, cultural explorers, desert trekkers, and architectural historians.

## Official & Planning

Essential official government portals, tourism boards, entry requirements, and cultural heritage guides.

- [Visit Morocco (Official Tourism Portal)](https://www.visitmorocco.com/en) - Official website of the Moroccan National Tourism Office (MNTO) featuring regional guides, medina highlights, and travel itineraries.
- [Consular Portal of Morocco (Ministry of Foreign Affairs)](https://www.diplomatie.ma/) - Official government portal for visa entry regulations, passport guidelines, and diplomatic missions.
- [UNESCO World Heritage Sites in Morocco](https://www.visitmorocco.com/en/morocco/unesco-world-heritage-site) - Official guide detailing Morocco's protected historical medinas, Roman ruins, and cultural monuments.

## Transportation

Navigating high-speed rail networks, intercity trains, bus companies, and domestic air travel.

- [ONCF (Moroccan National Railways)](https://www.oncf.ma/) - Official national railway operator managing Al Boraq high-speed trains (connecting Tangier, Rabat, and Casablanca) and intercity Al Atlas lines.
- [CTM Bus Services](https://www.ctm.ma/) - National long-distance coach network connecting major cities, towns, and rural regions across Morocco.
- [Supratours](https://www.supratours.ma/) - National bus company operating connecting routes to destinations beyond the rail network, including Southern desert regions.
- [Royal Air Maroc](https://www.royalairmaroc.com/) - National flag carrier operating international flights and domestic routes through Mohammed V International Airport (CMN) in Casablanca.

## Destinations & Regions

### Marrakech & South

- [Visit Marrakech (Official Regional Portal)](https://www.visitmarocco.com/en/travel/marrakech) - Official visitor guide for exploring Jemaa el-Fnaa square, historic souks, palaces, and the Atlas foothills.
- [Jardin Majorelle Official Portal](https://jardinmajorelle.com/) - Visitor information for the iconic botanical garden, Berber Museum, and Yves Saint Laurent memorial site in Marrakech.

### Fez, Meknes & Imperial North

- [Visit Fez (Official Regional Guide)](https://www.visitmorocco.com/en/travel/fez) - Visitor portal for exploring Fes el-Bali, ancient tanneries, traditional craft workshops, and Islamic architecture.
- [Volubilis Archaeological Site Info](https://www.visitmorocco.com/) - Official guide to visiting the remarkably preserved Roman ruins located near Meknes.

### Casablanca & Rabat

- [Casablanca Tourism Portal](https://www.visitmorocco.com/en/travel/casablanca) - Official visitor guide for the Hassan II Mosque, art deco architecture, and coastal Corniche.
- [Rabat Tourism Board](https://www.visitmorocco.com/en/travel/rabat) - Official travel hub for Morocco's capital, featuring the Kasbah of the Udayas and Hassan Tower.

### Chefchaouen & Rif Mountains

- [Chefchaouen Tourism Portal (The Blue City)](https://www.visitmorocco.com/en/travel/chefchaouen) - Official guide for exploring the famous blue-washed mountain town nestled in the Rif range.

### Sahara Desert & Oases

- [Sahara Desert Bivouacs & Erg Chebbi Guide](https://www.visitmorocco.com/) - Official tourism resource for planning desert camel treks, luxury desert camps, and visiting Merzouga or Zagora dunes.

## Safety & Cultural Etiquette

Practical advice on emergency contacts, bargaining customs, local clothing norms, and tipping etiquette.

- [Moroccan Emergency Services (Police 19 / Gendarmerie Royale 177)](https://www.visitmorocco.com/) - Direct national emergency contact numbers for immediate police assistance and public safety.
